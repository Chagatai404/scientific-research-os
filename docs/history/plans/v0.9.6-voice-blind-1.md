# Voice blind tests: English lecture paragraph (round 1, then round 2)

**Outcome after round 2 (2026-10-17): the default voice is Kokoro-82M with a style blend of 60% `af_heart` and 40% `af_sky`, speed 0.95, spoken sentence by sentence with 0.5 s of silence between sentences.** `af_sarah` in place of `af_sky` is the alternate; the researcher heard them as on par. Round 2 is at the end of this file; round 1 follows.

**Checks on the rater.** The anchor scored 2, the lowest of the seven, and the two copies of af_heart scored the same (difference 0), so the ratings separate a poor voice from the rest and are repeatable. They do not separate af_heart from af_bella, which tied at 4. One rater, one paragraph, one listening session; "acceptable" is the researcher's word for the best of them, not "natural".

**Reading.** af_heart is the best of this set and the only one that earned a "yes" on any copy. Every note on the top two voices is about pace and pauses, not timbre, so the next round tunes delivery before trying other voices: per-sentence synthesis with set silences between sentences, slower speed, and a blend of voice styles. af_bella is out (booms). Male and UK voices are out for now. Cost of Kokoro on this CPU is a real-time factor of 0.71 to 1.04, which is the limit for live sentence-by-sentence playback and is measured further in round 2.

**Checks.** The anchor scored 1 and the repeat matched (difference 0), so the ratings are usable. Five of six candidates scored 5, so the stars no longer separate them (a ceiling effect); the notes carry the ranking: sky blend, then sarah blend (on par), then the plain heart variants; round 1's delivery was too fast; 0.7 s pauses were too long. Variant b changed speed and pauses together, so which of the two was too much is not known. One rater, one 29 s paragraph; a twenty minute lecture was not tried.

**Speed on this CPU (measured, same run, no other heavy job).** Sentence by sentence the real-time factor was 0.43 to 0.58 (whole paragraph in one call had been 0.71 to 1.04 in round 1), first sentence ready in 1.5 to 1.8 s, and a simulated streamed playback had no stall for the sky variant. One earlier run showed a 34 s first sentence (cold start or a busy CPU), so a live page must warm the model before the lecture starts. Model: 82M parameters, 325 MB file, no GPU.

**Open before shipping a product (a personal installation is fine):** Kokoro's weights are Apache-2.0 per its model listings; the English phonemizer it uses on Windows (`espeakng-loader`, espeak-ng) has its own licence, which must be read before the voice is bundled in an app. Kokoro has no Turkish voice, so Turkish keeps its current providers.
