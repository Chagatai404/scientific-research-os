# Study Source

Read `references/SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

1. Record bibliographic/source metadata.
2. Identify why the source is credible and what role it plays:
   - evidence,
   - reference,
   - review,
   - teaching aid.
3. Separate:
   - source claim,
   - evidence/method,
   - source interpretation,
   - your interpretation.
4. Capture exact page/section/equation/figure/timestamp where possible.
5. List assumptions and scope.
6. Identify what the source does NOT establish.
7. Mark equations that require separate derivation.
8. Link claims to active research questions/hypotheses.
9. For papers, look for available code/data and a minimal reproduction path.
10. If a key claim matters to research, ask the source-verifier to independently verify it.
